The Agency

Your Advisors

Specialists by orbit — each one bound to a single stretch of the Saturn system, and to your confidence.

Halo Estates is a fictional showcase. The advisors below are invented for the world of the site — there is no one truly standing by, no line to ring, and no viewing to book. Consider it placeholder text with a name and a good coat.

How an introduction works

We place nothing on a public board. A member tells their advisor the orbit they have in mind — a ringfront terrace, a moon-side estate, a deck above the weather — and the advisor answers with what is quietly available. Every residence is matched to a person before it is ever named aloud.

Specialists by orbit

R

Rhea Calloway

Ringfront Specialist · B Ring

Knows the B Ring shoreline platform by platform, and can tell you which terraces catch ring-rise before the tram even runs. Placed the first stabilized villa anchored to the ring in 2271.

T

Tomas Enke

Moon-side Estates · Titan & Enceladus

Our man on the moons. Tomas measures a home by its walk to the ice geysers and the length of its equinox shadow — and will not show you a Titan estate he would not winter in himself.

I

Iva Sorensen

Penthouses · Observation Decks

Iva handles the top of the market, quite literally — observation-deck penthouses where the rings fill the glass. Discreet to a fault, and unhurried in a way that only the highest floors allow.

H

The Halo Desk

Concierge · Every orbit

Not sure which orbit is yours? The desk listens first and matches you to the right advisor after — the nearest thing this showcase has to a front door.

Every advisor here is fictional, and every introduction imaginary — you may enjoy the view of the rings without one. To reach the (equally fictional) desk, see Contact.
