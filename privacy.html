Privacy Notice

What Halo Estates would know about you — and how quietly it would keep it.

Halo Estates is a fictional showcase. There is no agency, no ringfront villa, and no data collected — this page is placeholder text standing in for a real privacy notice, written in the spirit of the world rather than the letter of the law.

What we would gather

Were Halo Estates real, we would note the orbits you lingered on, the residences you saved to your favourites, and whether you searched "ring view" at an hour that rather suggests you mean it. Today, none of that leaves your own browser.

Discretion, always

Ownership across the outer worlds is a private matter — it is one of our three principles, and it would extend to whatever we learned of you. No introduction made without your word; no viewing spoken of beyond your advisor.

Cookies & crumbs

Any residences you favourite are kept locally, in your own browser's memory — the way a good concierge holds a preference in mind without ever writing it down. Clear your browser data and they are forgotten entirely.

Your choices

Decline the Halo circle, keep your favourites to yourself, or simply drift on to the next orbit. Nothing here follows you home across the rings.

Reaching us

For everything a real notice would send to a real address: there isn't one. This is a demonstration site — please, enjoy the view of the rings.
