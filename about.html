Our Story

Founded the year the rings opened

When the first stabilized platforms were anchored to Saturn's B Ring in 2271, a home among the rings was the privilege of a handful of pioneers. Halo Estates was founded to represent them — quietly, and with an obsession for the extraordinary.

Five years on, we broker the finest ringfront villas, moon-side estates, and observation-deck penthouses across nine moons and three rings — always by introduction, never by listing board.

What Guides Us

Three principles

I

By introduction

We list nothing publicly. Every residence is placed privately, matched to a member before it ever reaches a board.

II

Obsessed with place

Ring-rise orientation, equinox shadow lines, transit proximity — we know every platform in the system by name.

III

Discretion, always

Ownership across the outer worlds is a private matter. Our advisors are bound to it absolutely.
