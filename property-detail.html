The Residence

Suspended on a stabilized ringfront platform within Saturn's B Ring, Aurelia is a study in weightless luxury. Floor-to-ceiling crystal glass wraps every principal room, framing the rings as they drift past in slow, luminous bands. The residence was conceived as a single sculptural gesture — curved cast-stone volumes, brushed-gold seams, and interiors finished in bone, alabaster, and warm oak.

A private ice terrace cantilevers over open ring space, while the double-height great room opens onto a sculptural stair that seems to float against the ringlight. Every suite faces ring-rise; the master wing is oriented for the annual equinox shadow, when the rings cast a knife-thin line across the whole sky.
