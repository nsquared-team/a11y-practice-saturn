Semantic HTML: demo pages

Five teaching pages built for the mastermind, hanging off the Halo Estates fixture but deliberately not in its navigation. Each one is clean except for the single thing it is there to show, so a live scan isolates the point instead of burying it.

Slide 6

Three controls, one keystroke

A native button, a link wearing button styling, and a div with role and tabindex. Identical on screen, all three pass a scan, one of them works. An event log records whether each keypress actually activated anything.

Open the demo

Slide 11

Headings that do not track the document

A property page whose visual hierarchy is obvious and whose heading levels are noise. Reveal the outline, then hide the page and read the outline on its own, which is the test from slide 12 without a screen reader.

Open the demo

Slide 16

Every cell knows its own headers

The same comparison data twice: once as the layout table the compare page ships, once with caption, scope col and scope row. A cell inspector shows what is left for a screen reader to announce in each.

Open the demo

Slide 18

The save toggle as shipped

One property card with the homepage save toggle untouched, on an otherwise clean page, so a scan has exactly one thing to find instead of thirty five.

Open the demo

Slides 19-20

The save toggle after a reasonable fix

The same control with aria-label and tabindex added and the click-only handler left alone. The scan comes back clean. Tab reaches it. Space does nothing.

Open the demo

A note for anyone who finds these later

These are not part of the Discover Saturn fixture. The fixture lives at the site root and carries a catalogue of seeded WCAG failures on purpose. These pages are the opposite: they are as correct as I could make them, apart from the one control or one structure each is demonstrating.

Do not add them to sitemap.xml and do not count them in fixture scan results. See demos/README.md in the repository.
