Step one: scan it. It flags.

One property card, lifted off the Halo Estates homepage with its save toggle exactly as shipped: a div with role="button", no accessible name, no tabindex, and a click handler.

Everything else on this page is already correct, so a scan has exactly one thing to find. An icon-only control with a role and no name is a markup fact, and machines are genuinely good at markup facts.
