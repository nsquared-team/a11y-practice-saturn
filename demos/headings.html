Slide 11

The real failure: headings that do not track the document

Below is a property page. Look at it. The hierarchy is completely obvious: one title, three sections, two subsections under the first two. Nothing about it looks broken.

Now pull up its heading outline. The levels underneath that clear visual hierarchy are noise. This is not a skipped level. This is structure that is conveyed visually and is not programmatically determinable, which is 1.3.1 Info and Relationships, Level A.

The test from slide 12, without a screen reader

Headings in the specimen document below, in source order, indented by their level. Read this list on its own and try to describe the page from it.

    Same page, same words, same design. The only change is that the levels now encode what the eye already knows.

    Specimen page

    B Ring · Ringfront Flagship

    Aurelia

    A ringfront flagship on the outer edge of the B Ring, held in the same family since the first survey season and offered now for the first time.

    The residence

    Eight thousand two hundred square feet across three levels, arranged so that every principal room faces the ring plane.

    Interior

    Five bedrooms, six baths, a double-height observation hall, and a kitchen finished in pale Enceladus stone. The master suite occupies the whole of the upper level.

    Grounds and terraces

    Two pressurised terraces and a zero-gravity infinity pool cantilevered over the shoreline of ring particles.

    The setting

    The outer B Ring is the most stable of the bright ring neighbourhoods and the closest to the Titan corridor.

    B Ring position

    Full ring view, uninterrupted, with the planet rising across roughly two thirds of the orbital day.

    Getting to Titan

    Two hours and twenty four minutes by private ring tram, with a berth included in the sale.

    Viewings

    By introduction only. Advisors arrange a full orbital day on site, timed to ring rise.
